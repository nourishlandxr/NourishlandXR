A preview of the actual welcome scene, fruit assets, gravity and Zinnia’s spoken guidance. Captions remain visible.

Drag a fruit from the basket to lift it. Hold O while dragging to open supported fruit, then release it onto the plate. Drag other objects to grab them. Pick up the remote, then release the mouse. Space flies forward; B reverses; left/right turns; up/down changes height. Fly through three rings, then land. Press O while holding the seed packet to open it.
