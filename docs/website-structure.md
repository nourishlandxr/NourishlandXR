# Nourishland website structure

The public preview introduces an early-stage educational project and invites co-development of one functional pilot. It does not sell a ready-to-deliver pilot service.

## Navigation

- Education: Food Forests & Solarpunk; Immersive Nature Learning.
- Platform: Creator; Explorer; Demos & access.
- The pilot: a small plot, reviewed learning package, access tools and evaluation.
- About: purpose, current stage and long-term direction.
- Collaborate: educators, students, universities, site hosts and development/funding partners; real email contact.

## Homepage sequence

Interactive botanical discovery die and clear introduction; two learning directions; proposed learner journey; Creator/Explorer; current development status; collaboration invitation.

## Presentation

User-supplied logo and botanical patterns; forest green, blue-grey, terracotta and teal; warm mineral backgrounds, restrained relief, generous image areas and compact standard icon controls. Concept imagery is labelled. Future photography and platform screenshots replace the corresponding figures.

## Current versus future

Existing website learning material and browser demonstrations are previews. The complete pilot, reviewed packages and reliable end-to-end learner journey are development milestones. Generated Guides/Adventures, shared publishing and the wider connected ecosystem remain future directions.

The separate wheel development files remain unchanged. The public homepage uses the existing botanical die.
