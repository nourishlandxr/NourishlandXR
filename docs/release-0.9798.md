# V0.9798 — Stable learning cells and a returning wheel close view

The hero again shows the Nourishland name beside the original emblem, plus Education and Platform links. The compact corner shell remains on the inner public pages. The emblem crop is restored; its image file is unchanged.

Opening any Education cell keeps the orb and all four cells at the same position and size. On wide layouts, the information uses the free space beside the selected side. On narrower screens it overlays the fixed scene, with a visible close control and destination action. The introductory copy now connects food forests with growing needs, seasonal changes and plant relationships, and explains why information linked to a living plant is useful.

The Food Forests wheel retains its slow continuous spin. Hover and keyboard focus highlight the three illustrated choices without changing the camera. Selecting Story, Learning guide or Hillyards first approaches the artwork and reveals a short introduction with a separate action to continue. Close or Escape returns to the overview. Switching choices preserves the spin phase. The previous white menu cards have been removed.

The camera fits the visible outline of the botanical artwork, including leaves and roots, into each view. This allows a visible approach without cutting off the wheel or cancelling the zoom with a matching camera-distance change. Desktop uses a companion introduction beside the wheel; phone and tablet layouts place it below. The complete seven-chapter story and guide content are preserved.
