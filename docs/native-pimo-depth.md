# Native PIMO content

The chooser retains Blue Quandong, Finger Lime and Lemon Myrtle. Each now has authored content in all six existing compass branches. Deeper ecology opens through Food Forest → Native forest → rainforest setting → Role in nature → Wildlife relationships.

| Plant | Species-specific exploration | Reused illustration files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blue Quandong | Restoration canopy, cassowary and water dispersal, regional fruit visitors, multi-seeded stone, mature-tree space | blue-quandong-rainforest.jpg, blue-quandong-seed.jpg |
| Finger Lime | Dainty Swallowtail host, eggs and caterpillar observations, thorny understorey, juice vesicles, seedling versus grafted plants | finger-lime-rainforest.jpg, finger-lime-seed.jpg |
| Lemon Myrtle | Flowering event, flower visitors, capsule development, aromatic leaf, cuttings and sheltered cultivation | lemon-myrtle-rainforest.jpg, lemon-myrtle-seed.jpg |

These six B&W illustrations came from the general updates work and already exist in `app/assets/pimo-cell-illustrations`. The three existing colour illustrations supply identity/leaf cells. No new image generation, downloads or animated objects are introduced. Images remain labelled as illustrations.

Botanical claims retain their source IDs. Observation tasks have `local_observation` evidence; a flower visit does not establish pollination, and a missing fruit does not identify its eater. Cassowary examples are geographically qualified. English, Portuguese and Dutch content is supplied together.

Sources: NSW Flora Online, Australian National Botanic Gardens, Rainforest Rescue, Wet Tropics Management Authority and Sunshine Coast Council. Direct links are retained in each PIM's source list.
