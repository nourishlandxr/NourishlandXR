## Agreed guide purpose

The owner chose **Understand a food forest**, with the opening:

A food forest brings edible and useful plants into a landscape inspired by a natural forest.

The guide now has four views: Living system / Layers / Design / People. The repetitive Learning view and duplicate expert slideshow are removed from this guide.

## Agreed human experience

The owner clarified that human use belongs in food-forest design: beautiful, diverse, multi-crop environments with places for people to interact, rather than a focus on productivity alone. They then emphasized curiosity, reconnecting with nature, feeling calm, reading a book, resting and noticing an unfamiliar insect.

The People scene now uses **Rest / Connect / Discover**. Its central message is: “Beauty, diverse crops and spaces to rest, connect and discover belong in the design.” The illustration is a concept scene, not a claimed photograph of a real place. Copy describes an inviting experience and makes no medical outcome claim.
