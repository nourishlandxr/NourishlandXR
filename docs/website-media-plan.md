# Nourishland website media plan

The website should show one coherent journey through Nourishland's own visual language. Generic nature imagery can establish a place, but the story is carried by Totems, Orbs, PIMOs, Zinnia and the Living Map.

## Narrative sequence

1. **Observe** — a real plant or place fills the frame. The interface stays quiet.
2. **Explore** — a Plant Orb gathers connected knowledge around the subject.
3. **Open** — a PIMO reveals cells for evidence, relationships, notes and learning paths.
4. **Reflect** — the learner connects what they observed and explains a pattern.
5. **Map** — discoveries return to a Living Map of the whole place.

The short Demo film should follow this sequence in 18–24 seconds: Totem, Plant Orb, opened PIMO, Living Map. Use a muted captioned loop; offer sound as a user-controlled option.

## Visual rules

- Use botanical cell patterns as compact square crops, short dividers or quiet corner details.
- Do not repeat a full pattern as the main image across several sections.
- Use one strong image per idea and let large, direct text lead the page.
- Label concept illustrations honestly. Reserve photography for real places, people and device use.
- Keep Solarpunk as the design philosophy across Nourishland: natural and futuristic, ancestral and modern, with technology serving living systems.
- Keep Android Field visible but secondary. It proves outdoor access; it is not the central promise of the website.

## Capture checklist

- Zinnia guiding one observation or reflection moment, portrait crop.
- Student closely observing one plant in place, landscape crop.
- Creator Desktop Studio showing one well-organised pilot project, 16:10 crop.
- Android Field showing one task at the real plot, 9:16 crop.
- Hillyards wide establishing photograph.
- Hillyards close plant observation with a hand, label or learner for scale.
- Demo screen recording that contains the four-shot journey and no unfinished debug interface.
