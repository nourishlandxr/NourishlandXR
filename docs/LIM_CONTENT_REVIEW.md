# LIM Phase 9 content review

## Review result

- Canonical LIM cells reviewed: **97**
- Generic placeholder cells rewritten: **61**
- Existing cells lightly edited for clarity, context or suitable length: **32**
- Existing cells retained without wording changes: **4**
- Cells requiring human factual confirmation: **0**

The four retained entries are **Layers**, **Seed**, **Soil**, and **Observation**. Their existing explanations were already plain, useful and appropriately cautious. Every other previously authored entry was adjusted only where it improved topic specificity, learning value, safety context or the distinction between repeated titles.

All content remains general LIM learning material. It does not describe the plant currently visible in a project, and it does not change Plant Information Mesh data.

## Face-by-face review

### Climate and Place

The 25 retained source cells now distinguish broad climate labels from local conditions. Repeated topics such as rainfall and growth have branch-specific explanations. Climate observations connect naturally with soil moisture, shelter, exposure, seasons and plant choices without presenting climate labels as guarantees.

### Living Landscapes

The 20 retained source cells describe layers as flexible design tools. Canopy, understorey, light, shade, mature form, function and yield now explain how structure changes relationships within a place. The content avoids requiring every landscape to contain every layer.

### Plants and Life

The 13 retained source cells cover identity, characteristics, propagation and soil in general terms. Identification content keeps uncertainty visible. Propagation methods describe the concept and variables without becoming instructions for one plant.

### Place and Observation

The 11 retained source cells separate direct observation from interpretation. Place, photographs, specimens, dates and conditions now retain the context needed for later comparison.

### Uses and Making

The four retained source cells and their parent face connect harvest with practical knowledge. Food and other uses include proportionate reminders that identification, preparation and suitability matter. No recipe, dosage, diagnosis or treatment advice is included.

### Origins and Culture

The five retained source cells and their parent face distinguish geographic origin from present range. Cultural content asks for sources and context and does not assign a practice to an unspecified country, culture or community.

### Wildlife and Relationships

The seven retained source cells and their parent face include habitat, pollinators, soil life and companion relationships. The text acknowledges competition, disease and uncertainty rather than implying that every interaction is beneficial.

### Discovery and Pathways

The eight retained source cells and their parent face support questions, methods, evidence, problems, actions and learning records. Free exploration remains the current experience. Future optional pathways are described as arrangements of the same cells, not as a required sequence.

## Overlapping topics

Several visible titles intentionally repeat in different contexts:

- **Rainfall** appears under Subtropical and Tropical.
- **Growth** appears under Tropical climate and Place observations.
- **Learning** appears under Place and Note.
- **Soil life** appears under Food-forest Ecology and Plant Soil.

These are meaningful overlaps rather than duplicates. Phase 9 keys companion content by stable ID, so each repeated title now has a distinct explanation suited to its branch.

## Future guidance plan

The LIM should retain free exploration while offering orientation in three gentle layers:

1. **First contact:** introduce one highlighted face with a short reason to open it, then let the explorer choose another face.
2. **Contextual connections:** after reading a cell, offer two clearly explained neighbouring ideas in the companion panel, including a Back action and an always-visible return to free exploration.
3. **Optional pathways:** later, group existing stable IDs into short goals such as reading a microclimate or tracing flower-to-pollinator relationships. A pathway should show progress and allow exit at any time without hiding cells or changing the mesh.

This approach provides direction without turning the LIM into a compulsory linear tutorial. No pathway interface or runtime behaviour was added during Phase 9.

## Human review notes

No current cell requires factual confirmation because the new copy stays at concept level and avoids unsupported plant-specific, medicinal or cultural claims. A future content round should involve relevant knowledge holders before adding named cultural practices or traditional knowledge, and subject specialists before adding species-specific safety guidance.
