# Website foundation

Lead visitors through one message in three steps:

1. **Why:** Plants and living systems have complex needs and relationships. Useful information can be difficult to find and connect.
2. **What:** Nourishland connects plant knowledge to real plants and places through interactive guides.
3. **How:** Choose a plant or place, open its guide and learn through observation, images and activities.

End this introduction with one primary action: **Try the demo**.

Use literal headings, short explanations and useful visuals. Let the artwork carry the imaginative character. State development status briefly and accurately.

Keep Food Forests, Immersive Learning, the story, Hillyards and Creator/Explorer available as optional detail. Preserve their graphics and interactions without making them required reading before the core explanation.

Add depth when it answers a visitor's question. Keep the Why, What and How introduction simple as the project evolves.
